# Ideas worth stealing from monfeur.art

Why that site reads as "a real designer, not AI": it has a **point of view, a voice, restraint, and craft in the transitions** — not just nice cards. Here's what to take, and honestly, what to leave (because your targets are fintech/product, not an avant-garde art studio).

## Take these

1. **Cinematic preloader → reveal.** Dark burgundy screen, a live % counter, and project cards *fanning/stacking into place* as it loads, then it resolves into the calm site. This one move screams "bespoke, high-craft." 
   → *Ours:* a short loader where your case screens slide into **gold frames** (or frames assemble) over a dark field with a % count, then reveal the light gallery. Keep it <2s and skippable.

2. **Dark → light transition.** The drama lives in the *dark* intro; the *work* lives on a calm *light* canvas. This elegantly resolves your dark-vs-light tension: **dark for atmosphere moments (loader, About), light warm-paper for the working gallery.**

3. **A literary "my approach" manifesto.** A section with a giant *italic serif* statement ("When form begins to speak for itself"). This is pure personality through words + type. 
   → *Ours:* you have an unfair advantage here — your book *No Water — A Designer's Confession*. Pull a line or two as your manifesto statement. That's a voice no AI and few designers have.

4. **Scroll-driven text reveal.** Paragraph words colour in from grey → dark (with a single accent word) as you scroll, like the text is being *read*. Subtle, senior, memorable.
   → *Ours:* apply to each case's opening line; accent the key metric or verb in gold.

5. **Tiny tracked uppercase kickers.** Every section is labelled with a small letter-spaced label (`МОЯ УСТАНОВКА`). Cheap, and instantly "designed."
   → *Ours:* `01 · DESIGN LEAD`, `THE PROBLEM`, `THE RIGOR` — we already lean this way; commit to it.

6. **A minimal project index.** Case names as a right-aligned, tracked-uppercase list that highlights on scroll/hover (1MICE · OLK · KELMA · ASTRA GAZOLINE…). Doubles as nav and as a "body of work" signal.
   → *Ours:* an index of the three cases + craft pieces; hovering one could preview its framed screen.

7. **Right-side dot progress + section anchors.** Quiet, always-there orientation. Pairs perfectly with your **traveling eye** — the eye *is* your progress indicator.

8. **Ruthless restraint + one accent.** Near-greyscale, enormous whitespace, faint paper texture, and a *single* red accent (echoed in the loader and the scroll dot). 
   → *Ours:* the same discipline with **gold as the one accent** (frames + one word per statement), everything else ink on paper.

9. **Poetic marginalia.** Tiny literary fragments in the margins as texture ("крылья были, все в пыли"). Adds soul.
   → *Ours:* sparing fragments from *No Water* in the margins of the About page.

## Leave these (honest calibration for your targets)

- **Full art-experiment obscurity.** monfeur is a pure art-direction site — it can be cryptic. **Revolut / Exness / Fundraise Up recruiters need the work legible and the impact obvious in 20 seconds.** Borrow the *craft signals*, but keep cases clear, captioned, and outcome-first. Atmosphere frames the work; it never hides it.
- **Loader that blocks.** A heavy multi-second loader can annoy a busy recruiter. Keep ours short, elegant, and skippable.
- **All-mystery navigation.** Keep a normal, obvious nav in addition to any artful index.

## Net: what makes it "not AI" — and how we get there
Voice (your book), restraint (one accent, whitespace), and craft in motion (loader, text-reveal, framed transitions). We already have the frames, the gaze, the palette, and the tokens. Adding **the loader, the manifesto voice from *No Water*, the scroll text-reveal, the kickers, and the case index** is what will tip it from "polished" to "unmistakably a senior designer."
