# Case visuals — curation plan (what to show, and why)

The governing rule: **every exhibit must earn its place for HR, for the Head of Design, or both.** Nothing decorative, nothing raw-dumped.

- **HR / recruiter (≈20-second scan):** looking for *scale, impact, "shipped," breadth, seniority signals.* Needs one instant "wow" per case and a legible outcome. Dense spec sheets mean nothing to them.
- **Head of Design (deep read):** looking for *decisions, trade-offs, rigor, systems thinking, craft.* Wants to see how you think, not just final pixels. Process artifacts are gold — but only if curated and captioned, never dumped.

Structure per case: **1 hero "wow" visual (HR)** + **2–3 depth exhibits (Head of Design)** + short captions. Curate ruthlessly.

## The meta-principle (applies to all three)

A senior portfolio shows **judgment about what to leave out.** Three cases, each = one instant win for the recruiter + a curated depth story for the Head of Design. Everything else is cut. Volume ≠ seniority; *selection* = seniority.
